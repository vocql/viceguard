    How the math works

    XP to next level = INT(120 + 8·L + 0.01·L²). Chat time assumes one XP message every cooldown, nonstop, averaging 25–45 XP per message. Voice time assumes 15–25 XP per minute. Event multiplier: 1x (staff events run 1x–200x and divide every count and time).

    //How XP Works

    Earning, boosts & events

    Messages
    25–45 XP each, one XP message per cooldown (60s at start, down to 15s at level 400). Messages under 4 characters do not count unless they have an attachment or sticker.
    Voice
    15–25 XP per minute in a voice channel. Muted, deafened or alone all still count. The AFK channel does not.
    AFK mode
    /afk earns 10–20 XP per minute even while offline or out of voice. Run it again to turn it off.
    XP boosts
    Permanent multiplier from +5% (level 10) up to +100% (level 800). Applies to message, voice and AFK XP.
    XP events
    Staff can run timed events from 1x up to 200x. They stack with your level boost.
    No-XP channels
    Staff can switch any text or voice channel to earn no XP.
    Level curve
    XP to next level = INT(120 + 8·L + 0.01·L²). Level 800 needs 4,355,944 total XP.
